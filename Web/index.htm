Reinforcement learning is the art of translating the "trial and error" paradigm to artificial intelligent systems. These pages explain and demonstrate how a neural network learns (without a teacher !) to play Abalone, a game very similar to chess but less complex.

Malin - Play Abalone against a Neural Network !
Snapshots, download and user guide of a intelligent computer game designed for MsWindows. For Free!

Reinforcement Learning in Theory and Practice.
A short introduction to reinforcement learning (neuro-dynamic programming) and its theoretical foundations. C++ pseudo code examples demonstrate its application to a self-learning neural network. A master's thesis covering these (and game-playing) issues in detail is available in Postscript.
