Malin - An Abalone Playing Program

 This software brings the game Abalone to your personal computer ... come and make the gloomy experience of losing against a purely artificial intelligent system !

Malin can be played by zero to four human players and includes a variety of computer players, its strongest based on a multi-layer feed-forward neural network (perceptron). To move, just drag and drop your marbles, Malin takes care nobody is cheating !

Algorithms

Note that the applied reinforcement learning algorithm enables a neural network to learn to play Abalone (almost) completely independently of any human teacher. The strongest network effectively carried out about 26.000 games against itself in order to adjust its originally randomized synapses. This new approach in combination with highly sophisticated game-searching techniques (iterative alpha-beta negamax, quiescence search, killer-move and hash tables) resulted in a surprisingly strong Abalone player.
