First Steps

Have a look at the screen-snapshot below. The largest part of the Malin window is dedicated to the game board. From the Players pane on the upper right side we know that the gray marbles belong to player Human and that he has already lost 1 marble (although he is a very slow player!). The history pane below lists the 24 plies (making 12 complete moves) which have already been played. Finally pay attention to the status bar in the bottom line - for the moment Malin is idle and waits for you to make a move.

To make a move, mouse-click on the desired marbles (up to three) - this will highlight them.  Then try to move them, just as you would do with any other window: Mouse-click one of the highlighted marbles, tear it (mouse button still down) in the desired direction and release finally the mouse button. Did not work out? You can cheat by using the menu item Move - Take Back to undo your move ...

If you want to sleep over the next move, use the menu items Game - Save and Game - Open respectively. By default Malin proposes you to challenge a computer opponent, but you can use Game - New to setup a new game with two, three or four players, each of them being human or one of dozens of different computer players. By the way, Malin is a fully multithreaded application (multi-tasking), so there is no problem in interrupting Malin while it is thinking about the next move.
