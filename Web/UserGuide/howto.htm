Malin - User Guide

How to ... ?

How do I know if a player is a computer or human player ?
The only human player is called Human, all other players are computer players. However, the status bar always tells you whether Malin expects you to move or does the job itself.

Why do many computer players not accept three or four player games ?
Because playing against more than one player requires different programming techniques. All really good Malin computer players are exclusively optimized for two player challenges, but you can always try the Aba or Boub computer player families.

What are these strange codes in the history pane ?
Each entry in the History list box, corresponds to one move and records when it was played, whose turn it was and which marbles were selected. See the figure Malin board notation to understand the move notation.

How do I get advice of an expert computer player ?
There is no menu entry like "Give advice", but you can always use the Game - Players utility to change your (Human) player into a computer expert, let him make one move and change back to the Human player.

How about having several instances of Malin running at the same time ?
No problem, your CPU will be occupied, that is all.

Does anything bad happen if I close the Malin window while it is busy ?
No, Malin cancels thinking and shuts itself down correctly.

What is left on my system, after I have run the remove utility ?
The program files and the entry in the system registration are deleted, only the four dynamic libraries stay in your Windows system directory.
